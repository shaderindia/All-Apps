Professional Income & Shift Differential Guide

Understand standardized pay intervals, compounded differential mechanics, and legal overtime standards.

1. Standard Pay Interval Equations

Converting hourly wages into standard weekly, monthly, and annual compensation utilizes the following payroll mathematics:

Weekly Gross Pay = Hourly Rate × Hours Worked per Week
Annual Gross Pay = Weekly Gross Pay × 52 Weeks
Monthly Gross Pay = Annual Gross Pay ÷ 12 Months

2. Compounded Shift Differentials

In manufacturing, healthcare, and engineering environments, overnight and weekend shifts receive premium multipliers. When shifts overlap (e.g., Night Shift on a Saturday), SHADER7 compounds these multipliers:

Effective Hourly Rate = Base Rate × Weekend Multiplier × Night/C-Shift Multiplier

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my hourly rate calculated?

Your hourly rate is calculated using your base salary and the fixed base hours you specify: Hourly Rate = Base Salary / Fixed Base Hours. For example, a 4,000 RON base salary across 160 fixed base hours yields exactly 25.00 RON/hour.

How do the weekend, night shift, and C shift multipliers work?

Multipliers scale your base hourly rate for specific hours worked. The weekend multiplier scales weekend hours. The night shift and C shift multipliers scale hours logged under those shift designations. If you log night hours on a weekend, both multipliers compound together to guarantee accurate differential tracking.

What are the standard monthly hours assumptions?

A standard full-time work month assumes 160 to 168 hours of labor (40 hours per week, 8 hours per day). You can freely adjust the "Fixed Base Hours" field to match your specific union agreement or employment contract.

Is my financial information stored or uploaded to servers?

No. The SHADER7 salary calculator is 100% client-side. All calculations, daily hours, employee data, and PDF statements are generated locally in your browser memory and saved to your device's private localStorage.

How do I download a PDF salary statement?

Once you fill in your hours for the month, click the Generate Report button. An A4 statement preview will open with your employee details and itemized shifts, which you can immediately save as a PDF or high-resolution JPG.
