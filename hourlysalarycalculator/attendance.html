Printing tips

Get a crisp, usable page

  1. Use landscape for two or three months when you want wider handwriting boxes.
  2. In the print dialog choose A4, Actual size or 100%, and turn off browser headers and footers.
  3. Use the JPG when sharing with a print shop; use PDF for the most reliable A4 size.
