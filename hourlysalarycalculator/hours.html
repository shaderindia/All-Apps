How to calculate your hours

  1. Choose the month, then enter your hours or select a shift on each working day.
  2. Review the total and the weekday, weekend, and shift breakdowns. Blank days are unlogged; zero hours means a day off.
  3. Use Save This Month to keep the log in this browser. Generate Report opens a preview for PDF or JPG export.

Entries use half-hour increments from 0 to 24 hours. Enter the working time you want to count, after any excluded breaks. OT is a label for hours you identify as overtime; the app does not apply an overtime threshold.

Unsaved months stay available while this page is open. Save each month before closing. Employee and company names are optional.
