Organize your digital paperwork with Shader7 tools

Most digital clutter is paperwork: phone photos of documents, three versions of a resume, receipts in your downloads folder and payslips in your email. When a form suddenly asks for "a PDF of your ID, a 100 KB photo and last month's payslip", the search begins.

This guide sets up a simple system for personal paperwork and shows which Shader7 tool produces each file in a clean, consistent format. Everything runs in your browser, so sensitive documents stay on your device.

Step 1: A folder structure you will actually use

Keep it shallow. Three levels is plenty:

Documents/
  01-ID-and-photos/
  02-Career/
    CV/
    Applications/
  03-Money/
    Payslips-and-hours/
    Receipts-issued/
    Shared-bills/
  04-Home/
  05-Work-projects/
  00-Inbox/
  • 00-Inbox is where every new download lands. Empty it once a week.
  • Numbers in front keep the folders in a fixed order.
  • Year folders inside Money (2026, 2027…) make tax time easy.

Step 2: One file-naming pattern

Use the same pattern everywhere:

YYYY-MM-DD_what_who-or-where.ext

Examples:

  • 2026-11-03_passport-photo-35x45_A4-sheet.pdf
  • 2026-11-10_CV_template6_text.pdf
  • 2026-11-30_salary-report_November.pdf
  • 2026-11-18_receipt_INV-0042_ClientName.pdf

Dates first means files sort by time automatically. Avoid spaces and words like "final" or "new".

Step 3: Make each document with the right tool

Scans and multi-page documents → Image to PDF

Phone photos of a lease, certificate or ID front and back are hard to send and easy to lose. Image to PDF turns them into one file:

  1. Add photos: up to 50 JPG, PNG or WebP images, 30 MB each.
  2. Move pages earlier or later, rotate or remove any page, or use Reverse Order.
  3. Set page size (A4, Letter or Legal), orientation (auto per photo, portrait or landscape), margin, JPEG quality and output DPI (150 is compact, 300 is print quality).
  4. Choose Fit whole image or Fill page, a white or black background, and Add page numbers if useful.
  5. Name the PDF using your pattern and press Create PDF.

Upload-ready photos → Photo Compressor

When a form needs a photo or scan under a size limit, use Photo Compressor with the exact KB target and dimensions. Save the compressed copy next to the original with the limit in the name, for example _100KB.jpg.

ID photos → Passport Photo Maker

Keep the original photo, the print-sheet PDF and a single digital photo in 01-ID-and-photos. Next time you need one, it is ready. Make them with Passport Photo Maker.

Resumes → CV Banao

Keep one master resume and export tailored versions per application. CV Banao gives you an image PDF for printing and a text PDF for online applications; save both, with the template and version in the file name.

Receipts you issue → Receipt Maker

Save every receipt from Receipt Maker as a PDF in Receipts-issued/2026, and export CSV monthly for a running income list.

Hours and pay → Hourly Salary Calculator

At month end, download the PDF or Excel report from the Hourly Salary Calculator and file it in Payslips-and-hours next to your official payslip. Comparing the two each month catches payroll mistakes early.

Shared bills → FairShare

Download the FairShare PDF report after each trip or monthly house bill so everyone can refer to the same record.

Step 4: A weekly ten-minute reset

Once a week:

  1. Empty 00-Inbox: rename and move each file, or delete it.
  2. Clear your downloads folder.
  3. Close browser tabs you are keeping "for later"; bookmark the few that matter.
  4. Check desktop icons: no more than a handful.
  5. Back up the Documents folder (see below).

Step 5: Back it up

Browser tools that save drafts in local storage, such as CV Banao and Receipt Maker, are convenient but are not a backup. Always download the final PDF or CSV into your folders. Then follow the 3-2-1 idea:

  • 3 copies of important files,
  • on 2 different types of storage (for example your laptop and an external drive),
  • with 1 copy off-site (a cloud drive or a drive kept somewhere else).

Step 6: Keep your tools within reach

Install Shader7 on your phone and computer with Install app on the home page, or your browser's Add to Home Screen. With the tools a tap away, it is easier to create a document correctly the first time than to fix it later.

Privacy reminders

  • Prefer tools that process files on your device for ID documents and financial records. Shader7's tools do.
  • On shared computers, clear saved drafts and downloads when you finish.
  • Encrypt or password-protect backups that contain ID documents.

A document-ready kit

Keep these files ready in your folders so you are never caught out by a form:

  • A digital passport-style photo (JPG) and a print sheet (PDF), made with Passport Photo Maker.
  • A compressed copy of that photo under 100 KB and under 50 KB, made with Photo Compressor.
  • One PDF each of your ID (front and back on one page), address proof and key certificates, made with Image to PDF.
  • Your current CV as both a text PDF and an image PDF from CV Banao.
  • Your last three months of salary or hours reports from the Hourly Salary Calculator.

Update the kit once a year, or whenever something changes.

Common organisation mistakes

  • Too many folders. If you hesitate about where something goes, the structure is too complicated.
  • Descriptive names without dates. "Lease" is hard to find among ten leases; "2026-09-01_lease_Flat3" is not.
  • Keeping only browser drafts. If you clear your browser or change device, drafts in local storage are gone.
  • Never deleting. Duplicates and old drafts make the right file harder to find.

Frequently asked questions

How many images can I combine into one PDF?

Up to 50 photos, each up to 30 MB, in the Image to PDF Converter.

What DPI should I use for scanned documents?

150 DPI is compact and readable on screen. Use 300 DPI if the PDF will be printed or needs fine detail.

Should I keep the original photos after making a PDF?

Keep originals of important documents such as IDs and certificates in case you need a different size or format later. Delete duplicates of everyday receipts.

Are my documents uploaded when I use these tools?

No. Image to PDF, Photo Compressor, Passport Photo Maker, CV Banao and Receipt Maker all process your files in your browser.

What is the best file-naming pattern?

Start with the date as YYYY-MM-DD, then what the document is, then who or where. Files then sort by date automatically.
