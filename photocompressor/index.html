Compress images

Photo compression questions, answered.

Can I compress to 100 KB?

Choose the 100 KB preset for JPG or WebP. The app aims to stay under the limit and tells you when your dimensions prevent it from reaching the target.

Will my photo keep its shape?

Resizing preserves proportions. Enter both width and height and enable cropping if you need a specific shape.

Why is PNG different?

PNG uses lossless encoding, so the target KB setting does not apply. Resize the image or try WebP for a smaller file with transparency.
