A resume has two readers: software that scans it, and a person who skims it for a few seconds. CV Banao, the free resume builder from Shader7, gives you six A4 templates with a live preview so you can see exactly what both readers will get. There is no login, and your resume stays in your browser.

This guide explains how to pick a template, fill in the form well and export the right kind of PDF.

Filling in the form

Each template has its own form, but the sections are similar. Using Template 6 as an example:

  • Personal Information: full name, headline or profession, email, phone, address, and optional date of birth and nationality. Include only what is normal in the country you are applying to.
  • Profile Photo: Choose Photo, then Adjust Crop to drag and zoom (slider, mouse wheel or pinch), and Save Crop. Leave the photo out where it is not expected, for example in the US and UK.
  • Education and Training: Add Education for each qualification. In the Europass template, add the EQF level (for example 6 for a bachelor's degree, 7 for a master's).
  • Work Experience: Add Work Experience for each role, newest first.
  • Skills: general and soft skills, and digital and technical skills, separated by commas or a pipe (|).
  • Language Skills: mother tongue, plus other languages with a CEFR self-assessment (A1 to C2) for listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing.

Fit it on the page

Every template previews an exact A4 page and warns you when the content no longer fits on one page. Use the template's fit control (Auto Adjust in Templates 1 to 4, Auto Fit in Templates 5 and 6), or shorten descriptions. A one-page resume suits most people with under ten years of experience; two pages are fine for longer careers.

Save, load and start over

  • Every template saves your work automatically in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back later.
  • Template 6 adds explicit Save Resume and Load Resume buttons, Reset Demo to fill the form with sample data, and Clear Saved to remove your data from the browser.

Writing bullets that get read

Recruiters skim. Each bullet should show what you did and why it mattered:

  1. Action: start with a verb. "Built", "reduced", "led", "trained".
  2. Method: the tools or approach. "using Python and SQL", "by rewriting the onboarding guide".
  3. Outcome: a real, verifiable result. "cut report time from 2 days to 3 hours".

Weak: Responsible for customer emails. Better: Answered 60+ customer emails a day and wrote 25 help-centre articles that reduced repeat questions by about a third.

Describe your work in your own words and use the terms from the job posting where they are true for you. Repeating keywords unnaturally rarely helps and reads badly to a person.

Applicant tracking systems (ATS)

Many employers run resumes through an ATS that extracts text into fields. Systems vary, and no template can guarantee how every one reads a file, but these habits help:

  • Use standard headings: Work Experience, Education, Skills.
  • Keep dates and job titles consistent and easy to find.
  • Avoid charts, skill bars and text inside images. A parser cannot read them.
  • Watch columns. Two-column layouts look good, but some parsers read straight across the page and mix the columns. If an application needs a machine-readable resume, check the text order (see below) or use a simpler layout.

Choosing the right PDF export

CV Banao offers two exports, and the difference matters:

Export What it creates When to use it
Download A4 PDF An image-based PDF that looks exactly like the preview Emailing a person, printing, portfolios
Print / Save text PDF A PDF with selectable, searchable text, made through your browser's print dialog (choose "Save as PDF") Online applications and any ATS upload

Quick check: open the text PDF, select all the text and paste it into a plain text editor. If the order makes sense, a parser will probably read it correctly. If columns are mixed up, try a single-column template.

If an employer asks for a DOCX file, use a word processor for that application.

Before you send it

  • Your name, email and phone number are correct and professional.
  • Dates, job titles and company names are accurate and consistent.
  • Every bullet starts with a verb and most include a result.
  • Spelling and grammar checked, ideally by someone else too.
  • You opened the exported PDF at full size and proofread it.
  • The file name is clear, for example FirstName-LastName-CV.pdf.

Which template should you choose?

  • Applying in the US, UK or Canada, or through a large online portal: a simple layout without a photo. Template 2 or 4 with the photo left out works well; export the text PDF.
  • Applying in Europe or for academic or public-sector roles: Template 6 (Europass-inspired), with EQF levels and the CEFR language grid that European recruiters recognise.
  • Creative or tech roles: Template 3's dark sidebar or Template 4's colour header can help you stand out, as long as the text PDF still reads in the right order.
  • Traditional sectors (banking, government, manufacturing): Template 1 or 5.

If you are unsure, make two versions. CV Banao saves your data per template in your browser, so switching layouts does not take long.

Common resume mistakes

  • Duties instead of results: "Responsible for reports" says less than "Built a weekly sales report used by 12 managers".
  • Inconsistent dates: mixing "Jan 2024" and "01/2024" looks careless to a reader and confuses parsers.
  • An unprofessional email address: use a simple firstname.lastname style address.
  • Too much personal data: leave out ID numbers, marital status and religion unless the employer or country specifically requires them.
  • One resume for every job: reorder bullets and adjust the headline for each application.

Frequently asked questions

Is CV Banao really free?

Yes. All six templates are free, there is no login, and there is no watermark.

Which export should I use for an online application?

Print / Save text PDF, which keeps your text selectable and searchable. Download A4 PDF creates an image PDF that looks identical but is harder for software to read.

Will my resume pass every ATS?

No template can promise that, because systems differ. Standard headings, simple formatting and a text PDF give you the best chance. Always follow the format the employer asks for.

Where is my resume stored?

In your browser's local storage on your device. On a shared computer, clear it afterwards: use Clear Saved in Template 6, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Should I include a photo?

It depends on the country and industry. Photos are common in parts of Europe, Asia and the Middle East, and usually left out in the US, UK and Canada.
