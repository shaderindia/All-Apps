A passport or visa photo does not need a studio. With a decent phone photo, a plain wall and a printer (or a print shop), you can make a full sheet of correctly sized photos at home. Passport Photo Maker by Shader7 handles the fiddly parts: cropping to the exact size, replacing the background, tiling copies onto a page and exporting a file that prints at the right scale.

This guide follows the app's three steps and ends with printing tips and a checklist.

Before you start: take a usable photo

The app can crop and change the background, but it cannot fix a blurry or badly lit photo. Aim for:

  • Even light on the face, from a window in front of you or two lamps at 45°. Avoid overhead lights that shadow the eyes. The lighting guide explains simple setups.
  • A plain, light background with a bit of space behind you, so no shadow falls on the wall.
  • The camera at eye level, about 1–1.5 m away, using the normal (not wide) lens.
  • A neutral expression, eyes open, face square to the camera, glasses off unless your authority allows them.
  • The whole head and shoulders in frame, with space above the hair.

Step 1: Upload and edit your photo

  1. Choose a photo or drag and drop it. JPG, PNG, WebP and other browser-supported images up to 25 MB work. The photo stays on your device.
  2. Crop: drag to position the photo, pinch or scroll to zoom. Keep the whole head inside the crop frame with some space above it.
  3. Rotate left / Rotate right to straighten the image, and Reset crop to start the crop again.
  4. Background: press Remove background. The app separates you from the background using an AI segmentation model that runs in your browser. Then pick White or a Custom color.
  5. Inspect the edges around hair and clothing at a high zoom. Fine flyaway hair is the hardest part for any background remover, so tidy hair before the photo if you can.

Press Next: size & layout when you are happy with the crop.

Step 2: Size and layout

Photo size

Choose a preset:

Preset Common use
35 × 45 mm Many passports and visas in Europe, the UK, India and elsewhere
2 × 2 inches US passport and visa photos
50 × 70 mm Some visa and residence permit photos
300 × 400 px Online application forms that ask for pixel dimensions
Custom dimensions Anything else

A preset sets the dimensions only; it does not check whether your photo meets an authority's rules. Always confirm the size, head height and background colour required for your document.

Paper size and count

  • Paper size: A4 (210 × 297 mm), 4R (4 × 6 inches) or Custom paper.
  • Photo count: choose how many copies, or turn on Fill the page to fit as many as possible.

Advanced settings

Open Dimensions, spacing & borders for full control:

  • Measurement unit: pixels, millimetres or inches.
  • Print resolution (DPI): 300 by default (range 72–1,200).
  • Photo dimensions and custom paper dimensions.
  • Spacing & margins: horizontal gap, vertical gap, top margin and left margin.
  • Show cutting guides: thin lines to cut along.
  • Add photo borders: an outline around each photo.

Reset returns to the 300 × 400 px default with standard spacing.

Step 3: Preview and download

The preview shows the full sheet. Zoom with Fit sheet, 1.5×, 2× or 3× to inspect detail. Then choose:

  • PDF · exact print size: the best choice for printing. The PDF page matches the paper size, so the photos print at the right physical size.
  • JPG image: with Standard (smaller file) or Best quality. Handy for print kiosks and online forms.
  • PNG image: lossless, larger file.

Very large sheets are limited to 20 megapixels for a safe export; if you hit the limit, lower the DPI or choose a smaller paper size.

Printing at the right size

  • Print the PDF at 100% or "Actual size". "Fit to page" or "Scale to fit" shrinks the photos and makes them the wrong size.
  • Use photo paper for the best result. Matte or satin is often preferred because it does not reflect glare.
  • For a print shop or kiosk, take the JPG and ask for the paper size you set (for example 4 × 6 inches) with no cropping or borders added.
  • Measure one printed photo with a ruler before cutting the rest.
  • Cut along the guides with a paper trimmer or a sharp knife and a metal ruler.

Privacy

The photo is processed in your browser. The background-removal model is downloaded to your browser the first time you use it, and then your image is processed locally; it is not uploaded to a Shader7 server.

Prefer your phone? There is also a Passport Photo Pro Android app, linked from the download step.

Final checklist

  • The size matches what your authority asks for.
  • The head is centred, straight and the right size within the frame.
  • The background colour matches the requirement and has no halos around the hair.
  • The face is evenly lit, with no shadows or glare.
  • You printed at 100% and measured one photo.

Worked example: two US passport photos on 4 × 6 paper

  1. Upload your photo, crop with the whole head in frame and some space above the hair, and press Remove background, then White.
  2. In Size & layout, choose 2 × 2 inches and 4R (4 × 6 inches).
  3. Turn on Fill the page. The preview shows exactly how many 2 × 2 inch photos fit with your margins and gaps.
  4. Turn on Show cutting guides.
  5. In the preview step, choose JPG image with Best quality for a photo kiosk, or PDF to print at home at 100%.

A 4 × 6 print usually costs very little at a pharmacy or photo kiosk, which makes this one of the cheapest ways to get official-size photos.

Troubleshooting

Problem Likely cause Fix
Photos print too small Printer scaled the page Print the PDF at 100% or Actual size, not Fit to page
Halo around the hair Busy or similar-coloured background Retake against a plain wall with more light from the front
Head looks too small or too big Crop zoom Zoom in or out in Step 1 until the head fills the frame as required
Colours look off Mixed lighting Use daylight or matching bulbs; avoid coloured walls reflecting on skin
Export blocked as too large Sheet over 20 megapixels Lower the DPI or use a smaller paper size

Photos for online forms

Many online applications ask for a digital photo with set pixel dimensions and a maximum file size, not a print sheet. Use the 300 × 400 px preset (or custom pixel dimensions), download a JPG, and if the file is still too big, run it through Photo Compressor with the KB limit the form gives you.

Frequently asked questions

Which photo sizes does the app support?

Presets for 35 × 45 mm, 2 × 2 inches, 50 × 70 mm and 300 × 400 px, plus custom dimensions in pixels, millimetres or inches.

Will the app tell me if my photo is accepted?

No. The app crops, sizes and prints the photo, but only the issuing authority decides whether it meets its rules. Check their current requirements before you submit.

How do I print the correct size?

Download the PDF and print it at 100% or Actual size. For JPG, use the same paper size you set in the app.

Can I make photos for online applications?

Yes. Choose the 300 × 400 px preset or custom pixel dimensions and download a JPG. If the form has a file-size limit, use the Photo Compressor afterwards.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping, background removal and the print sheet are all created in your browser.
