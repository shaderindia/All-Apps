Shader7 is a set of free browser tools for everyday paperwork, photos, money and precision machining. Everything runs in your browser, nothing needs an account, and you can install the site on your phone or computer for app-like access.

This page is a quick map of the whole suite: what each tool does, its standout features and where to learn more.

Photo and image

Passport Photo Maker

Make passport and visa photos at home. Crop and rotate your photo, remove the background with an AI model that runs in your browser, then choose a size (35 × 45 mm, 2 × 2 in, 50 × 70 mm, 300 × 400 px or custom) and tile copies onto A4, 4 × 6 in or custom paper with cutting guides. Export a PDF at exact print size, or a JPG or PNG. An Android app is also available.

Open Passport Photo Maker · Guide · Lighting tips

Photo Compressor

Get a photo under a size limit such as 50, 100, 200 or 500 KB, or any custom target. Choose JPG, WebP or PNG, resize in px, cm, mm or inches, crop before compressing, and compare before and after.

Open Photo Compressor · Guide

Image to PDF Converter

Combine up to 50 photos into one PDF. Reorder, reverse and rotate pages, choose A4, Letter or Legal, orientation, margins, JPEG quality, output DPI (72 to 600), fit or fill, background colour and page numbers. It works offline once loaded and uses no external libraries.

Open Image to PDF

Documents and career

CV Banao (resume builder)

Six free A4 templates, from a classic photo CV to a Europass-inspired layout with EQF levels and a CEFR language grid. Fill in a form with a live preview, crop your photo, and export either an image PDF or a text PDF for applicant tracking systems. Your resume auto-saves in your browser.

Open CV Banao · Resume guide · Interview prep

Receipt Maker

Professional receipts with five templates, a theme colour, your logo, line items, tax and discount, and 12 currencies plus custom. Export to PDF, JPG, JSON or CSV, print it, or copy a summary. Drafts autosave.

Open Receipt Maker · Guide

Money

Hourly Salary Calculator

Monthly pay from a daily shift log, with weekend, night, C-shift and Romanian public holiday rates, RON to INR conversion and Excel, PDF or JPG reports. It includes an Hours Only tracker and a printable Attendance Sheet maker.

Open the calculator · Guide · Budgeting

FairShare

Split trips, dinners and household bills with equal or weighted shares, exact-to-the-cent rounding and the fewest payments to settle. Share the result as text, PNG or PDF.

Open FairShare · Guide

Chat

Quick Private Chat (Ber of Chat)

Experimental peer-to-peer chat rooms for up to four people you know, joined with a room code. Includes direct call codes, a friends list, voice and video calls, message search and an option to hide your IP through relays. File sharing is disabled in the current version.

Open Quick Private Chat · Guide

What every tool has in common

  • Free, with no paywalls or sign-up.
  • Runs in your browser: files are processed on your device. See where your data goes for the details of each tool.
  • Works on phones and computers, and can be installed from the home page with Install app.

Ideas for using the tools together

  • Applying for a job abroad: passport photo → compress it for the online form → CV Banao → interview prep.
  • Freelancing: Receipt Maker for every payment → export CSV monthly → budget with the money guides.
  • Shift work: log shifts in the Hourly Salary Calculator → print an Attendance Sheet → split shared rent in FairShare.
  • Machine shop: model a fixture in 3D CAD Studio → check speeds in the milling planner → generate bolt holes with the PCD Generator.

Tips for getting the most out of Shader7

  • Install it on your phone for one-tap access to every tool.
  • Bookmark the tools you use most directly, for example the Hourly Salary Calculator or Photo Compressor.
  • Read the guide for each tool once; most include worked examples and troubleshooting tables.
  • Keep your own copies of finished files. Drafts saved in the browser are convenient, but they are not a backup.

Frequently asked questions

Are all Shader7 tools free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, paywalls or watermarks.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Every tool works without sign-up.

Can I install Shader7 as an app?

Yes. Use Install app on the home page, or your browser's Add to Home Screen or Install option.

Do the tools work offline?

Many calculators and editors keep working once the page has loaded. Features that need the network, such as live exchange rates, chat and the first download of the background-removal model, need a connection.

How do I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Use the Contact page.
