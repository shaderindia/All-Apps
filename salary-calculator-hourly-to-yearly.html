If you work shifts, your payslip rarely matches a simple "salary ÷ 12". Weekend hours pay more, night shifts pay more, some months have more working days, and if you send money home you also care about the exchange rate. The Advanced Hourly Salary Calculator by Shader7 was built for exactly this: you log each day of the month and it works out your pay, shift by shift.

It also has two companion tools: Hours Only for tracking time without pay, and an Attendance Sheet maker for printable forms.

How your pay is calculated

The calculator starts from your contract:

Hourly rate = Base salary ÷ Fixed base hours per month

For a base salary of 4,000 RON and 160 base hours, the hourly rate is 25.00 RON.

Then, for every day you log:

Daily pay = Hours × Hourly rate × Multipliers
  • The weekend multiplier applies to Saturday and Sunday hours.
  • The public holiday multiplier applies to Romanian public holidays (when holiday marking is on). If a holiday falls on a weekend, the higher of the holiday and weekend rates is used, never both.
  • The night shift multiplier applies to days set to Night.
  • The C shift multiplier applies to days set to C.
  • Night and C shift multipliers are multiplied on top of the weekend or holiday rate (for example, a weekend night shift uses weekend × night).

Monthly salary is the sum of all daily pay.

Example: hourly rate 25 RON, weekend 1.5×, night 1.25×. A 12-hour weekend night shift pays 12 × 25 × 1.5 × 1.25 = 562.50 RON. The same shift on a weekday pays 12 × 25 × 1.25 = 375 RON.

Step by step

1. Enter your details

  • Employee name and Company name (optional; they appear on reports and sync across the three tools).
  • Month.
  • Base salary (RON / month) and Fixed base hours per month, from your contract. Press Auto to set base hours to this month's working days × 8 hours (Monday to Friday, minus Romanian public holidays).
  • Weekend multiplier: 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× or 2×.
  • Night shift and C shift multipliers: 1× (no extra), 1.10×, 1.15×, 1.25×, 1.5× or 2×.
  • Public holiday multiplier: 1× (no extra), 1.5×, 2× (+100%), 2.5× or 3×.
  • Mark Romanian public holidays: on by default. The calculator knows Romania's 17 legal public holidays, including the moveable Orthodox Easter and Pentecost dates. Switch it off if they do not apply to you.

2. Log your days

Once you pick a month, the daily log appears as a List or a Calendar, with weekends and public holidays marked. For each day, tap a preset or pick hours and a shift. Use Quick-fill to fill the month fast:

  • Weekdays 8h
  • Weekdays 12h day
  • Weekdays 12h night
  • Weekends off
  • Clear month

The weekday quick-fill buttons leave public holidays empty, so add hours yourself if you worked one. Then adjust individual days, such as an extra Saturday or a day off. Remove all hours clears the log.

3. Read the results

The This month panel stays in view while you log and shows Total gross salary with its INR estimate, Hours, Days worked and Hourly rate, plus a breakdown of how your pay was built. If the base salary is missing, the app tells you so instead of showing a misleading zero.

4. Save and report

  • Everything is saved automatically in this browser; Save confirms it.
  • Preview A4 report opens a report with signature lines that you can download as PDF or JPG, or as an Excel workbook with the daily breakdown.
  • Share sends the calculator link to colleagues.

RON to INR and the currency converter

Many users earn in Romanian leu and support family in India, so the calculator shows an Approximate INR figure for your monthly salary. Turn on Auto-update from live rates to use the latest rate, or type your own rate (for example, the rate your transfer service offers).

The built-in Currency Converter uses daily reference rates from the Frankfurter API (EUR base). Enter an amount, pick two currencies and press Refresh rates for the latest figures. Remember that banks and transfer apps add fees and use their own rates, so the INR figure is an estimate.

Hours Only and the Attendance Sheet

  • Hours Only logs shifts without any pay data. Shift presets are A, B and C at 8 hours and Day/Night at 12 hours, in half-hour steps. It shows weekday, weekend, night, C-shift and OT hours, days worked and the average per working day, and exports a PDF or JPG report. OT is a label you choose; the app does not apply an overtime threshold.
  • Attendance Sheet creates a printable A4 form with one, two or three months on a page, in portrait or landscape, with employee name, company, ID, department and supervisor. Download it as PDF or JPG, or print it.

Converting hourly pay to yearly, monthly and weekly

If you just want a quick conversion for a job offer, these standard formulas help:

From To Formula
Hourly Weekly Hourly rate × hours per week
Weekly Yearly Weekly pay × 52
Yearly Monthly Yearly pay ÷ 12
Monthly Hourly Monthly pay ÷ monthly hours

At 40 hours a week, 25 per hour is 1,000 a week, 52,000 a year and about 4,333 a month. Note that 52 weeks × 40 hours = 2,080 hours, so a month averages about 173 hours, not 160. Use the base hours your contract states.

Tips for accurate results

  • Use your contract's base hours. They decide your hourly rate.
  • Log the hours you are paid for, after unpaid breaks.
  • Check how your employer combines premiums. This calculator multiplies the night or C shift rate on top of the weekend or holiday rate. If your employer adds them instead (for example +50% and +25% = +75%), the results will differ slightly.
  • Check holiday pay rules. Under the Romanian Labour Code, work on a public holiday is normally compensated with time off within 30 days, and paid with a bonus of at least 100% (2×) only if that is not possible. Set the holiday multiplier to match what you actually receive.
  • Use one browser. Your log saves automatically, but only in the browser and device you used.
  • Results are estimates before tax. Taxes and deductions are not included.

Worked example: a month of mixed shifts

Base salary 4,000 RON, 160 base hours (25 RON per hour), weekend 1.5×, night 1.25×, C shift 1.15×. In a 30-day month with no public holidays on the days you work, you log:

Days Shift Hours per day Multiplier Pay
12 weekdays Day 8 1 12 × 8 × 25 = 2,400
4 weekdays Night 12 1.25 4 × 12 × 25 × 1.25 = 1,500
2 weekdays C 8 1.15 2 × 8 × 25 × 1.15 = 460
2 weekend days Night 12 1.5 × 1.25 2 × 12 × 25 × 1.875 = 1,125

Total hours: 96 + 48 + 16 + 24 = 184. Total monthly salary: 2,400 + 1,500 + 460 + 1,125 = 5,485 RON.

Enter the same roster in the calculator and it should show 184 hours and 5,485.00 RON. If you then set the RON to INR rate, the approximate INR figure updates automatically.

Comparing two job offers

  • Use the calculator for each offer with that employer's base salary, base hours and multipliers.
  • Log the roster you would actually work, not an idealised one.
  • Compare monthly totals, then multiply by 12 for a rough annual figure.
  • Remember benefits not in the calculator, such as meal vouchers, transport and bonuses.

Common mistakes

  • Entering paid hours including unpaid breaks.
  • Using 160 base hours when your contract says 168 or 173.
  • Leaving the holiday multiplier at 2× when your employer gives time off instead of extra pay (or the reverse).
  • Logging on a different browser or device and wondering where last month went.

Frequently asked questions

How is my hourly rate calculated?

Base salary divided by fixed base hours per month. 4,000 RON and 160 hours gives 25.00 RON per hour.

How are weekend and night multipliers combined?

They are multiplied. A weekend night hour at 1.5× and 1.25× pays 1.875 times the base rate. On a public holiday, the higher of the holiday and weekend rates is used, and the night or C shift rate is multiplied on top.

Does it know public holidays?

Yes, Romania's 17 legal public holidays, including Orthodox Easter and Pentecost. They are marked in the log, use the public holiday multiplier, are skipped by the weekday quick-fill buttons, and are left out when Auto sets the base hours. You can switch holiday marking off.

Does the calculator include tax?

No. It shows gross pay based on your hours and multipliers. Check your payslip or tax authority for deductions.

Where is my data saved?

In your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and Hours Only logs are saved separately from salary logs.

Can I use a currency other than RON?

The salary fields are in RON with an INR estimate, but the currency converter handles other pairs. The hours and multiplier maths works the same for any currency.
