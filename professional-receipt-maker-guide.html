Freelancers, small shops, tutors and landlords all need to hand over proof of payment, and a handwritten slip rarely looks professional. Receipt Maker by Shader7 creates a clean receipt in your browser with your logo, itemised lines, tax and discount, ready to print or send as a PDF or image. There is no sign-up, and your draft saves itself as you type.

Receipt or invoice?

They are easy to mix up:

  • An invoice asks for payment. It is issued before you are paid and usually includes payment terms such as "Net 15" or "Due on receipt".
  • A receipt proves payment has been made. It is issued after payment and records how the customer paid.

Receipt Maker is built for receipts, but the same layout works as a simple invoice: change the title in your footer message or notes and leave the payment method as the expected method.

Step by step

1. Choose a template and colour

Tap a style and the preview updates instantly:

  • Classic: a monospaced, till-roll style.
  • Minimal: clean and modern with bold accents.
  • Corporate: structured, suited to B2B work.
  • Boutique: soft and decorative for shops and studios.
  • Dark: a dark theme for screen sharing.

Use Theme Color to match your brand.

2. Add your company information

  • Company Logo (optional): PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP, up to 2 MB. Click to upload or drag and drop; Remove Logo clears it.
  • Company Name, Address and Phone.
  • GST / VAT / Tax ID (optional): include it if you are registered.

3. Fill in receipt details

  • Receipt / Invoice #: for example INV-0001. Use a new, sequential number for every receipt.
  • Date.
  • Customer Name (for example "Walk-in Customer").
  • Cashier / Staff Name.

4. Add items

Press + Add Item for each line and enter the description, Qty and price. Quantities can be decimals, which is handy for hours worked (1.5) or weight. The preview updates as you type.

5. Totals and payment

  • Currency: USD, INR, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, SEK, NZD, RON, or Custom Currency with your own symbol or text (for example AED or R$) placed before or after the amount.
  • Payment Method: Cash, Card, UPI, Bank Transfer, Online Payment or Other.
  • Tax Rate (%) and Discount (%).
  • Footer Message, such as "Thank you for your business!" or your refund policy.

How the totals are calculated

Receipt Maker applies the discount before tax, which is how most tax systems expect it:

Subtotal = sum of (quantity × price)
Discount = Subtotal × discount %
Taxable  = Subtotal − Discount
Tax      = Taxable × tax %
Total    = Taxable + Tax

Example: two items totalling 1,000, a 10% discount and 18% GST. Discount = 100, taxable = 900, tax = 162, total = 1,062.

Exporting and sharing

Preview first, then choose:

  • Print: send it straight to a printer or "Save as PDF" from the print dialog.
  • Download PDF: best for email and records.
  • Download JPG: easy to share on WhatsApp or messaging apps.
  • Export JSON: a structured data copy of the receipt for your records.
  • Export CSV: line items for a spreadsheet or your bookkeeping.
  • Copy Summary: a text version to paste into a message.

Keep your logo under 2 MB for the best PDF and JPG output.

Autosave and privacy

The live preview shows Autosave Active: your current draft is kept in your browser's local storage, so a refresh or accidental close does not lose your work. Reset Form starts fresh. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

On a shared computer, press Reset Form when you finish so the next person does not see your customer's details.

What every receipt should include

  • Your business name, address and contact details.
  • Your tax ID, if you are registered for GST, VAT or similar.
  • A unique, sequential receipt number.
  • The date of payment.
  • A clear description, quantity and price for each item.
  • Discounts and tax shown separately, with the rate.
  • The total paid and the payment method.

Rules differ by country and by business type. If you are registered for tax, check what your tax authority requires on receipts and tax invoices.

Record-keeping tips

  • Save a PDF of every receipt in a folder per month or per year.
  • Export CSV regularly so your income records are in a spreadsheet, not only in individual PDFs.
  • Never reuse a receipt number, even if you cancel a receipt. Issue a new one for corrections.
  • Use the same currency your customer paid in, and note any conversion separately.

Worked example: a freelance design job in INR

  1. Choose the Corporate template and set the theme colour to your brand colour.
  2. Upload your logo and enter your business name, address, phone and GSTIN.
  3. Set Receipt # to the next number in your sequence, for example INV-0042, and today's date. Enter the client's name in Customer Name.
  4. Add items: "Logo design" × 1 at 12,000, and "Revision rounds (hours)" × 3 at 1,500.
  5. Choose INR, Bank Transfer, Discount 0% and Tax 18%.
  6. Check the totals: subtotal 16,500, tax 2,970, total 19,470.
  7. Add a footer such as "Paid in full. Thank you!" and press Download PDF.

Name the file with the date, receipt number and client so it is easy to find later.

Tips for different businesses

  • Shops and market stalls: use the Classic template for a till-roll look and Copy Summary to send a quick text receipt on WhatsApp.
  • Tutors and coaches: use one line per session with the date in the description, and quantity as the number of sessions.
  • Landlords: one line for rent and separate lines for utilities, with the rental period in the description.
  • Repair services: separate parts and labour, so the customer sees both clearly.

Common mistakes

  • Reusing receipt numbers or skipping them without a record.
  • Forgetting to show the tax rate and tax amount separately when you are registered.
  • Sending a JPG when a client's accounts team needs a PDF.
  • Using a large logo file that makes the PDF heavy; keep it under 2 MB.

Frequently asked questions

Is Receipt Maker free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of receipts.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP up to 2 MB. It appears on the preview and in the PDF and JPG.

Does it apply tax before or after the discount?

After. The discount is taken off the subtotal first, and tax is calculated on the discounted amount.

Can I use a currency that is not in the list?

Yes. Choose Custom Currency, type the symbol or code, and choose whether it goes before or after the amount.

Where is my data saved?

In your browser's local storage on your device, so your draft survives a refresh. It is not uploaded to Shader7.
