Where your data goes in each Shader7 tool

A tool-by-tool look at how Shader7 apps handle your photos, resumes, bills and chats: what is processed in your browser, what is saved on your device, which outside services are contacted, and how to clear it.

"Free online tool" often means "upload your file to someone else's server". For a passport photo, a resume with your address or a bill with your friends' names, that is a real privacy cost. Shader7 is built the other way round: the tools run in your browser, and your files are processed on your own device.

"Runs in your browser" is a good start, but you deserve the details. This guide goes tool by tool: what is processed locally, what is saved on your device, which outside services are contacted, and how to remove what is stored.

Three terms worth knowing

  • Processed in your browser (client-side): the calculation, image editing or PDF creation happens in JavaScript on your device. Your file is not sent to a server to do the work.
  • Local storage: a small storage area in your browser, on your device, that a site can use to remember your work, such as a draft resume. It is not uploaded, but anyone using the same browser profile could see it.
  • Third-party request: when a page contacts another service, for example to download a code library, look up an exchange rate or show an advert.

Photos: Passport Photo Maker, Photo Compressor and Image to PDF

These handle the most sensitive files, so they do all the image work locally. Passport Photo Maker's background removal uses an AI segmentation model that your browser downloads the first time you press Remove background; after that, your photo is analysed on your device. Photo Compressor and Image to PDF never need to send your images anywhere to work.

Documents: CV Banao and Receipt Maker

Both save your work in local storage so you do not lose it on a refresh. That is convenient on your own device, but on a shared or public computer you should clear it when you finish: use Clear Saved in CV Banao's Template 6, Reset Form in Receipt Maker, or clear the site's data in your browser.

Money: Hourly Salary Calculator and FairShare

Your salary, hours and shared bills stay in your browser. The Hourly Salary Calculator contacts the Frankfurter API for currency rates; that request asks for exchange rates and does not include your salary figures.

CNC tools

Speeds, feeds, coordinates, G-code and 3D models are all calculated in the browser. Nothing about your parts or programs is sent anywhere. 3D CAD Studio only keeps a project if you press Save JSON, which downloads a file to your device.

Quick Private Chat

Chat is the one tool that must use the network, because messages go to other people. It connects browsers directly with WebRTC where possible and encrypts the connection. A signalling service helps browsers find each other, and relay servers carry traffic when a direct connection is not possible. Messages are not stored on a Shader7 server. Delete My Data & Revoke Consent clears what the app stored on your device.

Adverts and site analytics

Some Shader7 pages show adverts or sponsored links, and the site uses advertising and analytics services. These load separately from the tools and may use cookies or similar technologies to collect standard browsing data. They do not receive the files you open in the tools, because those files are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How to check for yourself

You do not have to take anyone's word for it:

  1. Open the tool and your browser's developer tools (F12 on desktop), then the Network tab.
  2. Load a photo or fill in a form and press the main button.
  3. Watch the requests. Processing your file should not create an upload of that file.

You can also turn on airplane mode after a page has fully loaded: most calculators and editors keep working, which shows the work happens on your device.

How to clear stored data

  • In the tool: Reset Form (Receipt Maker), Clear Saved (CV Banao Template 6), Start a new bill (FairShare), Remove all hours and Clear month (Hourly Salary Calculator), Delete My Data (Quick Private Chat).
  • In your browser: open site settings for shader7.com and clear its storage, or use a private window, which forgets everything when closed.

Good habits with any online tool

  • Prefer tools that process files in your browser for ID photos, documents and financial data.
  • Use a private window on shared computers.
  • Do not paste passwords, bank details or ID numbers into tools that do not need them.
  • Download your result and keep your own copy; do not rely on browser storage as a backup.

Questions to ask about any online tool

You can use the same checklist for tools from any site:

  1. Does it need my file on a server to work? Image editing, PDF creation and calculations can all run in the browser.
  2. Does it ask for an account it does not need? Sign-ups create another copy of your data somewhere.
  3. What does the privacy policy say about uploads and retention? Look for clear statements, not vague ones.
  4. Which outside services does the page contact? The Network tab in developer tools shows this.
  5. Can I delete what it stores? Look for a clear reset or delete option.

Shared and public computers

Library, office and internet-café computers need extra care:

  • Use a private or incognito window, which clears local storage when closed.
  • Download your result, then delete it from the computer's Downloads folder.
  • Log out of any email or cloud account you used to send the file.
  • If you used a normal window, clear the site's data before leaving.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shader7 upload my photos?

No. Passport Photo Maker, Photo Compressor and Image to PDF process images in your browser. Passport Photo Maker downloads its background-removal model on first use, then works on your photo locally.

Why does my resume or receipt come back when I reopen the page?

CV Banao and Receipt Maker save drafts in your browser's local storage on your device. Clear them with the tool's reset option or your browser's site-data settings.

Do I need an account?

No. None of the Shader7 tools require sign-up.

Is Quick Private Chat end-to-end encrypted?

WebRTC encrypts the connection between peers where supported. When a relay is needed, traffic passes through a relay server, and signalling services help set up the connection. Messages are not stored on a Shader7 server.

Do adverts see my files?

No. Adverts load separately from the tools, and your files are never uploaded, so advertising partners cannot see them.

Use tools that keep your files on your device

Passport photos, resumes, receipts, bill splits and CNC programs are all created in your browser, with no account and no upload of your files to a Shader7 server.
