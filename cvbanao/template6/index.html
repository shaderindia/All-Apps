Official Europass Standard Format Guide

The Europass CV is one of the most widely recognized curriculum vitae formats across Europe and international corporations. It provides standardized clarity for recruiters and educational institutions:

1. Chronological Experience Format

Europass work experience entries combine your job role, employer organization, dates, and location in a prominent single title line (ROLE – COMPANY – DATES – LOCATION), immediately followed by concrete responsibilities and measurable outcomes.

2. European Qualifications Framework (EQF)

Under the Education and Training section, specify the EQF level corresponding to your degree (e.g. EQF Level 6 for Bachelor's, EQF Level 7 for Master's, EQF Level 8 for Doctorate) to assist international hiring committees in cross-referencing your credentials.

3. CEFR Language Self-Assessment Grid

Language abilities are evaluated using the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR): A1/A2 for Basic user, B1/B2 for Independent user, and C1/C2 for Proficient user across Listening, Reading, Spoken production, Spoken interaction, and Writing.
