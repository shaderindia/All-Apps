Before you export your resume

Choose a layout that suits the role and follow any format requested by the employer. Recruitment systems handle files differently, so no visual template can guarantee how every system will read it.

  • Check the basics: Confirm your contact details, dates, job titles, and section headings are accurate.
  • Review the PDF: Open the exported file, check the A4 layout at full size, and proofread every section.
  • Match the role: Describe relevant work and skills in your own words, without repeating keywords unnaturally.

Write useful experience bullet points

Start with the work you did, explain your contribution, and include a result when you can verify it. Specific examples help a reader understand your experience more quickly.

  1. Action: Name the task or project you completed.
  2. Method: Explain the tools or approach you used.
  3. Outcome: Add a real result or measurable change if available.

Common format questions

PDF or DOCX? Follow the job posting. Download A4 PDF creates a page image; Print / Save text PDF creates selectable text through your browser. Use another editor if DOCX is required.

Can I use columns? Columns can complicate machine reading. If an application requires a machine-readable resume, choose Print / Save text PDF and check the copied text order; use a simpler layout if needed.

Mastering the Resume Writing Process

Practical tips to make your CV clearer and easier to review.

Make your resume easier to parse

Applicant tracking systems vary. These habits can make an exported resume easier to review and parse:

  • Clear Headings: Stick to standard titles like "Work Experience", "Education", and "Skills" so the parser maps data correctly.
  • Simple Text Formatting: Avoid complex embedded charts, timelines, or graphical progress bars that confuse parser libraries.
  • Relevant Language: Describe the skills and experience requested in the job post when they genuinely apply to you.

Chronological vs. Functional Formats

Choosing the correct resume architecture defines how recruiters evaluate your professional progression and background:

  • Reverse-Chronological: Lists your most recent work first and helps readers follow your career progression.
  • Functional / Skills-Based: Best for career switchers, recent graduates, or individuals with employment gaps. Focuses heavily on skill clusters rather than specific dates.
  • Hybrid / Combination: Balances skills lists with a solid history section. Highly flexible and modern.

Writing High-Impact Bullet Points

Your job descriptions must go beyond listing basic daily tasks. They need to demonstrate quantifiable business results using strong action verbs:

  • Use Action Verbs: Replace passive phrases like "Responsible for managing" with strong verbs like Spearheaded, Optimized, Engineered, or Coordinated.
  • Quantify Results: Whenever possible, add metrics to show impact (e.g., "Boosted team productivity by 22% through automated tools").
  • The STAR Formula: Describe the Situation, Task, Action you took, and final Result achieved to provide clear context.

FAQ

Is CV Banao free?

Yes, it is completely free to use. You can create and download your resume as a PDF without any hidden fees or subscriptions.

Do I need an account to create a resume?

No, you do not need an account. You can start building your CV directly in your browser without any registration, and your progress is saved locally to your device's browser memory.

Which resume template should I choose?

Select Template 1 for a traditional photo CV, Template 2 for a blue two-column layout, Template 3 for a modern dark sidebar, Template 4 for a customizable color header, Template 5 for a classic maroon layout, or Template 6 for a Europass-inspired layout. Preview each sample before choosing.

Will every applicant tracking system read these resumes?

Download A4 PDF produces a page image. Print / Save text PDF uses the browser's print dialog to create a PDF with selectable text. Follow the employer's file instructions; DOCX is not supported.

Can I upload a custom photo to my resume?

Yes. All six templates include a photo upload control. Choose an image from your device and review its crop in the live preview.

Is my personal data and resume content secure?

Absolutely. CV Banao is a privacy-first web application. All resume construction and PDF compilation are executed directly on your client browser. No personal data, work histories, or profile photos are uploaded to any server or cloud database.
