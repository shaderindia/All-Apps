How Quick Private Chat by Shader7 connects you peer to peer

A guide to Quick Private Chat (Ber of Chat): creating and joining rooms of up to 4 people, direct chats with saved friends, group voice and video calls you can join or leave, the relay option that hides your IP, what is stored on your device, and troubleshooting.

Most chat apps route every message through a company's servers and keep your history there. Quick Private Chat by Shader7 (also known as Ber of Chat) takes a different approach: it uses WebRTC to connect browsers to each other directly where possible, for small rooms of people who already know each other. There is no account and no phone number, and messages are not stored on a Shader7 server.

It is an experimental app, so this guide also explains its limits and how to fix connection problems.

What you can do

  • Rooms of up to 4 people, joined with a 6-character room code.
  • Direct chats with saved friends using their call code (QPC-XXXXXXXX). They accept or decline each request.
  • Text chat with multi-line messages, delivery ticks, a typing indicator, an emoji picker and message search.
  • Group voice and video calls that each person joins or leaves on their own, with mic and camera controls.
  • Light and dark mode, and install as an app on Android, iPhone or desktop.

Getting started

Before you start

On first use you agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and enter your date of birth to confirm you are 19 or older. The check runs only on your device, and after the first time it collapses to a single line you can Edit. Enter a display name; this is what others see.

Create a room (host)

  1. Open the Create tab.
  2. Pick the Room size: 2, 3 or 4 people, including you.
  3. Press Create & host room.
  4. Share the room code with Copy code, or send the Invite link, which opens the app with the code filled in. Use a channel you trust.

As host, keep the app open: rooms work only while the host stays online. The button at the bottom of the room panel reads Close room; using it, closing the tab or losing connection ends the room for everyone.

Join a room

  1. Open the Join tab, or open the invite link you were sent.
  2. Enter the 6-character room code.
  3. Press Join room.

If the code is wrong or the host has gone offline, the app says so instead of waiting forever. If the room is full, you'll see that too. Rejoin last room appears under the code field after you've been in a room.

Friends and direct call codes

The Friends tab shows your direct call code, in the format QPC-XXXXXXXX. Copy it to share, or press New code to replace it (your old code stops working, so send friends the new one). To add someone, open Add a friend, enter their call code and a nickname and press Save friend. In a room, the person icon next to anyone in the People list adds them to your friends in one tap. Your friends list is saved on your device.

To chat with a friend, press Chat next to their name. They see a request with your name and call code and can Accept or Decline. They need the app open to receive it. A direct chat is just the two of you; when either person leaves, the chat ends.

Calls

Press the phone or camera icon in the header to start a voice or video call. Everyone else in the room gets a call screen with Join and Decline. If you decline, or arrive later, a green Join call button stays in the header for as long as the call is going. Each person leaves with the red button without ending the call for the others; the call ends when the last person leaves. Use Ctrl+Shift+M to mute, Ctrl+Shift+V for the camera and Ctrl+Shift+E to leave.

How the connection works

WebRTC is the technology browsers use for video calls. Quick Private Chat uses it through the PeerJS library:

  1. Signalling. When you create or join a room, your browser contacts a signalling service to find the other members and exchange connection details. This is like looking up a phone number; the signalling service does not carry your chat.
  2. Finding a route. Browsers use STUN to discover how to reach each other through home routers and firewalls (NAT).
  3. Direct connection. Where possible, browsers connect directly, and messages and calls travel between devices.
  4. Relay fallback. On strict networks, such as some offices, schools and mobile carriers, a direct connection is not possible and traffic goes through a TURN relay server instead.

WebRTC encrypts the transport between peers (DTLS for data, SRTP for audio and video) where supported.

The "Hide my IP" option

In a direct WebRTC connection, peers can see each other's IP addresses, which can reveal your approximate location. Turn on Hide my IP address to send all of your traffic through relay (TURN) servers, so other people only see the relay's address. Three things to know:

  • Relays can make calls slower.
  • Relay providers may still process connection metadata, such as IP addresses and timing.
  • If the relay service is unavailable, connections fail instead of falling back to a direct route. Turn the option off if you can't connect.

What is stored, and where

Data Where
Messages Exchanged between devices; not stored on a Shader7 server
Display name, last room, recent emojis Your browser's local storage
Friends list and your call code Your browser's local storage
Consent records Your browser; download with Download consent log

Use Delete my data & revoke consent at the bottom of the start screen to clear what the app stored on your device.

Using it responsibly

The app is for people you know. Do not use it for illegal, abusive, harmful or unsafe activity. Use the flag icon (Report a problem) in the chat header to send a report to the privacy and grievance contact. The Country legal checklist gives a product-readiness overview for your country code; it is not legal advice, and laws vary.

Troubleshooting

Problem Try this
"Room wasn't found" Check the code; make sure the host still has the app open
Friend didn't get your chat request They need Quick Private Chat open, and can't be in a room or another chat at the time
Connects on Wi-Fi but not mobile data (or the reverse) The network may block direct connections; try another network or turn on Hide my IP to use relays
Room ended suddenly The host closed the room or tab, lost connection, or their phone locked the browser
Can't connect with Hide my IP on The relay service may be down; turn the option off and try again
No audio or video Allow microphone and camera in the browser's site settings; close other apps using the camera
Messages arrive slowly Relayed connections are slower; a direct connection on the same network is fastest
App asks to agree again Local storage was cleared or you are in a private window

Install it as an app

  • Android Chrome: menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • iPhone Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Desktop Chrome or Edge: the install icon in the address bar, or menu → Install.

Tips for a smooth session

  • Let the host use the most stable connection. Because the room lives on the host's device, a laptop on home Wi-Fi is a better host than a phone moving between networks.
  • Keep the host's screen awake. Phones often pause browser tabs when the screen locks, which can end the room.
  • Share codes privately. Send room and call codes only to the people you want in the room.
  • Start with text, then call. Text uses the least bandwidth; start a voice or video call once everyone has joined.
  • Use headphones on calls to avoid echo.

Who it suits

Quick Private Chat is designed for quick, private conversations between a few people who already know each other: planning something with friends, a short call with family, or a quick chat with a colleague when you do not want to exchange phone numbers. It is not designed for large groups, public communities or long-term message history, and because it is experimental, it should not be relied on for anything critical.

Frequently asked questions

How many people can join a room?

Up to four, including the host.

Why did my room disappear?

Rooms exist only while the host is online. If the host closes the room or the app, or loses connection, the room ends and everyone returns to the start screen with a message.

If I leave a call, does it end for everyone?

No. Each person leaves on their own, and anyone who hasn't joined yet can still join from the header. The call ends when the last person leaves.

Are my messages stored on a server?

Messages are exchanged between devices and are not stored on a Shader7 server. Signalling and, when needed, relay services help set up and carry the connection.

Can I send files?

No. Quick Private Chat is for text chat and voice and video calls; it does not send files.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. You must be 19 or older. The age check is processed locally on your device.
