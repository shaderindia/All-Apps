Working from home gives you control of your day, and also removes most of the structure an office provided: fixed hours, a commute that separates work from home, and someone who notices when you work late every night. A good remote routine rebuilds that structure on purpose.

This guide sets out a simple weekly routine and shows where free Shader7 tools fit in: tracking time, billing, paperwork, shared costs and quick private calls. None of them need an account, and they all run in your browser.

Set up the basics

A defined workspace

Work from the same spot every day if you can, even if it is a corner of a table. When work ends, close the laptop and leave the spot. The physical boundary helps your brain switch off.

Core hours

Decide when you are reachable and share it with your team or clients, for example 9:30–13:00 and 14:00–17:30. Protect one block a day for focused work with notifications off.

Start and shutdown rituals

  • Start: check today's calendar, write the three things that must get done, open only the tabs you need.
  • Shutdown: log your hours, note tomorrow's first task, close everything.

Track your time honestly

Remote workers tend to underestimate the hours they work. The Hours Only tracker in the Shader7 Hourly Salary Calculator suite makes logging quick:

  1. Choose the month. Add your name and company if you want them on the report.
  2. For each day, enter hours in half-hour steps (after unpaid breaks) or tap a shift preset: A, B or C at 8 hours, Day or Night at 12 hours, or Off.
  3. Use Quick-Fill (for example Weekdays 8h) for a regular week, then adjust.
  4. Read your weekday, weekend, night, C shift and OT hours, days worked and average per working day.
  5. Save This Month and use Generate Report for a PDF or JPG.

Blank days are unlogged; zero means a day off. OT is a label you choose for overtime; the app does not apply a threshold, so you decide what counts.

Review the totals every Friday. If your average keeps creeping above your contract, that is a conversation to have early, before burnout.

If you are paid by the hour or have shift premiums, the full Hourly Salary Calculator turns the same kind of log into monthly pay.

Billing and paperwork for freelancers

Receipts and invoices

Use Receipt Maker to send a professional document for every payment: your logo, line items (quantities can be hours such as 7.5), tax and discount, and the client's currency. Download a PDF for the client, and Export CSV monthly for your records.

Documents clients ask for

Contracts, signed forms and ID scans often come as phone photos. Combine them into one ordered PDF with Image to PDF, and shrink large images for upload portals with Photo Compressor.

Keep your CV current

Remote roles attract many applicants. Update CV Banao every quarter with recent results while you still remember the numbers, and use the text PDF export for online applications.

Shared costs with other remote workers

Coworking day passes, team lunches when you meet up, or a shared office rented by a few freelancers all create small debts. Enter what each person paid in FairShare, use weights for people who used the space less, and send everyone the PDF with the payment plan.

Quick private conversations

For a quick call or chat with people you know, such as a co-founder or a small client team, Quick Private Chat creates a room for up to four people with a 6-character code. It connects browsers directly where possible and offers voice and video calls. It is experimental and the room lasts only while the host is online, so keep your team's main tools for day-to-day work and use it for quick, private conversations.

Protect your energy

  • Take real breaks. Stand up, look away from the screen and move every hour.
  • Keep a hard stop most days. Logging your hours makes overwork visible.
  • Talk to people. Schedule regular calls with colleagues that are not about tasks.
  • Separate devices or profiles for work and personal use if you can.

Common remote-work pitfalls

  • Always-on availability. Being reachable at every hour feels helpful but leads to burnout. Share your core hours and stick to them.
  • Unlogged time. Without a log, overtime disappears into evenings. Five minutes in Hours Only each day makes it visible.
  • Late invoicing. Sending receipts or invoices weeks later delays payment and makes records messy. Issue them on the day you are paid.
  • Paper chaos. Signed forms and scans scattered across your phone are hard to send. Turn them into one PDF straight away.
  • Isolation. Plan regular contact with colleagues or other freelancers, in person when you can.

Setting up a new remote job in one afternoon

  1. Agree your core hours and preferred contact channels with your manager.
  2. Set up your workspace and test your camera, microphone and lighting.
  3. Open Hours Only, enter your name and company, and start the month's log.
  4. Create folders for contracts, payslips and work documents, and save your signed contract as a PDF.
  5. Install Shader7 on your phone and laptop so the tools are always at hand.

Frequently asked questions

Can I track hours without entering a salary?

Yes. Hours Only logs days and shifts and gives totals and a PDF or JPG report without any pay data. Its logs are saved separately from the salary calculator.

Does the hours tracker calculate overtime automatically?

No. OT is a label for hours you mark as overtime. The app does not apply a threshold, because rules differ between contracts and countries.

Can I bill clients in their currency?

Yes. Receipt Maker supports 12 currencies plus a custom symbol or code placed before or after the amount.

Is Quick Private Chat suitable for team chat all day?

It is an experimental tool for small rooms of people you know, up to four, and rooms end when the host goes offline. It suits quick private calls and chats rather than a permanent team workspace.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser on your device. None of these tools upload your logs, receipts or documents.
